# Reading Concordances with Algorithms

## A training course in key corpus linguistics methodology

A one-week course taught at the [**DGfS/GSCL Fall School 2026**](https://cl-fallschool-2026.github.io/) on Computational Linguistics held at [UTN Nürnberg](https://www.utn.de/en/) on **21–25 September 2026**. Instructors: [**Stephanie Evert**](https://www.stephanie-evert.de/) and [**Michaela Mahlberg**](https://michaelamahlberg.com/) ([FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg](https://www.fau.eu/)).
